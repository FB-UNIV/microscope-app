# A Fiche prints each Vue principale's original photo with only its Échelle

**Status:** amends [ADR 0016](0016-fiche-one-sheet-per-tp.md): a Vue principale is no longer printed "rendered with its Treatments". The rest of ADR 0016 (one sheet per TP; name, Grossissement and Échelle; nothing else) and the layout of [ADR 0019](0019-fiche-three-vues-per-page.md) still stand.

At the second lab session (2026-10-19) a Fiche printed on the lab printer had the right layout, size and Échelle, but each photo carried its Treatments: the Levels and the phase map set on screen (#82). Those are for looking at a photo and for exports, not for the printed sheet. Decided: the Fiche prints the stored original with only its Échelle Treatment drawn on top (`fichePhoto()`). Levels, the phase map and any other Treatment stay on screen and in exports. The bar is the Vue's own Échelle Treatment, drawn where it is on screen. Considered dropping the Échelle too: the photo would have no scale on paper, which is why every calibrated Vue gets one (ADR 0017). Considered drawing the bar in the PDF from the Calibration instead: it could differ from the one on screen.

A calibrated Vue whose Échelle was removed prints without one, like an uncalibrated Vue. The bar is not drawn again from its Calibration: the Fiche prints what the Vue carries, and nothing else in the app draws an Échelle the Vue doesn't have. The Grossissement is still printed under it, and the app lists that Vue under *À vérifier* when the Fiche is written, so a removed Échelle is noticed before printing. Whether such a Vue should get its bar back on paper is a question for the teacher (#64). If so, `fichePhoto()` would add an `EchelleTreatment` built from `Vue::calibration()`.

**Consequences:** The Fiche no longer reads `Vue::render()`. `fichePhoto()` in the core picks the Échelle Treatments out of the pipeline, in order, on a copy of the original, so it is tested without Qt. `writeFichePdf()` prints that image. Nothing changes on screen, in exports or in the sidecar.
