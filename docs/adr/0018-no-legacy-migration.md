# No migration from the legacy app: the legacy importers are removed

**Status:** removes the legacy-import path of [ADR 0015](0015-calibration-tied-to-frame-size.md); the rest of ADR 0015 stands.

Phase 1 shipped importers for the legacy Department catalogs (`GMP1.txt`, `CTTM.txt`, …: 7 TP × 8 Échantillon slots) and for legacy view TIFFs with their calibration tag, so earlier years' TP structure and photos could be brought over. Decided: nothing is migrated. Departments, TPs and Échantillons are entered fresh in the new app, and the legacy photo archive stays read-only where it is. Both importers (`legacy_catalog`, `legacy_view`), their tests and the catalog fixtures are removed rather than kept unused. Considered importing legacy views on demand or in bulk: legacy file names carry only slot numbers whose meaning changed whenever a teacher edited a catalog, and reused slots had overwritten earlier photos (the root cause behind ADR 0004), so filing old photos under Échantillons would be guesswork.

**Consequences:** A Vue gets a Calibration only by being captured with one (ADR 0015). `docs/legacy/` stays: it documents the algorithms ported bit-exact (ADR 0012) and why the new design differs. Phase analysis and Dureté Vickers are unaffected, and their parity tests keep running on the 256×256 crops of legacy views in `tests/fixtures/legacy/` and, when given, on the legacy TIFF folder. The legacy notes in CONTEXT.md (Department codes, the 288.65 px/mm constant) remain as history. If migration is ever wanted again, the importers are in git history just before this change.
